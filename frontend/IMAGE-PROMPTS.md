# HandLoan.ai — image prompts for ChatGPT

Generate each image in ChatGPT in **landscape (1536 × 1024)**, then send them back. They'll be cropped,
compressed and dropped into `public/images/` under the file name shown. No code changes are needed.

## Paste this style block at the start of EVERY prompt

> Photorealistic editorial lifestyle photograph, shot on a 50mm lens, soft natural daylight, shallow depth of
> field, clean and uncluttered, calm and trustworthy mood. Subjects are Indian adults with natural, relaxed
> expressions (not over-excited). Subtle colour accents in soft teal-blue (#06A3DA) and fresh green (#34AD54)
> in clothing or props; otherwise light neutral tones. **No text, no letters, no numbers, no logos, no brand
> names, no watermarks. No cash, coins, currency notes, credit cards, gold or luxury items. No bank buildings.
> Any phone, tablet or laptop screen faces away from the camera or shows only a blurred, abstract interface
> with no readable text.** Landscape orientation, 3:2.

## Do NOT generate (send real files instead)

- **App screenshots.** Use real screenshots from the HandLoan.ai app, because Meta compares the ads, the website
  and the app.
- **Lender logos.** Only use logos that each lender has given you in writing.
- **RBI, ONDC, bank or government emblems.** Using these implies an endorsement and gets ads rejected.
- **Anything showing a "guaranteed" or "approved" stamp, cash or a person in distress.** Meta rejects these.
