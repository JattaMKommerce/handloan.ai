PART 6. EMI
======================================================================

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount paid every month until a loan is repaid. Each EMI covers part of the principal and the interest on the outstanding balance.
Formula: EMI = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = tenure in months.
A longer tenure means a lower EMI but more total interest. Keeping total EMIs within 40 to 50% of monthly income is a good rule of thumb.
Customers can calculate their EMI at handloan.ai/emi-calculator/ (it runs only in their browser).

Example EMIs at 14% per year (illustration only, excluding fees):
- Rs 50,000 for 12 months: EMI about Rs 4,489; total interest about Rs 3,872
- Rs 1,00,000 for 12 months: EMI about Rs 8,979; total interest about Rs 7,745
- Rs 1,00,000 for 24 months: EMI about Rs 4,801; total interest about Rs 15,224
- Rs 1,00,000 for 36 months: EMI about Rs 3,418; total interest about Rs 23,039
- Rs 2,00,000 for 24 months: EMI about Rs 9,603; total interest about Rs 30,462
- Rs 3,00,000 for 36 months: EMI about Rs 10,253; total interest about Rs 69,118
- Rs 5,00,000 for 36 months: EMI about Rs 17,089; total interest about Rs 1,15,197
- Rs 5,00,000 for 60 months: EMI about Rs 11,634; total interest about Rs 1,98,048
- Rs 10,00,000 for 60 months: EMI about Rs 23,268; total interest about Rs 3,96,095

PART 7. IMPORTANT LOAN TOPICS (GENERAL INFORMATION)
======================================================================

KEY FACTS STATEMENT (KFS): a standard summary that RBI-regulated lenders must give before you sign. It lists the loan amount, interest rate, APR, all fees and charges, the repayment schedule, the cooling-off period and the lender's grievance contact.

CREDIT CHECK / CIBIL: HandLoan.ai does not fetch credit reports, so sending an enquiry does not affect the credit score. The lending partner that reviews an enquiry may check the credit report, only with the customer's consent. General tips to build a healthy score: pay EMIs and card bills on time, keep credit card usage low, avoid many loan applications in a short time, and check your credit report for errors.

REPAYMENT: any loan from a lender is repaid to that lender directly. HandLoan.ai does not collect repayments.

COOLING-OFF: digital loans from RBI-regulated lenders have a cooling-off period, stated in the KFS, during which the borrower can exit by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR-based cost without penalty.

WITHDRAWING AN ENQUIRY: email help@handloan.ai to withdraw an enquiry and ask for the details to be deleted.
