PART 4. LOAN PRODUCTS
======================================================================

PERSONAL LOAN
- What it is: an unsecured loan (no collateral or guarantor) repaid in fixed monthly instalments (EMIs).
- Common uses: education and courses, wedding and family functions, medical expenses, home renovation and furnishing, travel, consolidating existing loans into one EMI.
- Loan amount: Rs 25,000 to Rs 25 lakh
- Repayment tenure: 12 to 60 months
- Interest rate: 10.5% to 16% per year (reducing balance), set by the lender based on the customer's profile
- Decision time: lenders typically decide within 24 to 48 hours after receiving complete documents. Timelines vary by lender and are not guaranteed.
- Typical eligibility (each lender has its own criteria):
  * Indian resident, usually aged 21 to 60 years
  * Salaried or self-employed with a regular income
  * Minimum monthly income usually Rs 20,000 to Rs 25,000 (varies by lender and city)
  * Credit score generally 750 or above
  * Salaried: usually 6+ months with the current employer and 2+ years total work experience
  * Existing EMIs ideally below 40 to 50% of monthly income
- Documents the lender may ask for: PAN card; Aadhaar (for eKYC, with consent) or another officially valid KYC document; bank statements for the last 3 to 6 months; salaried: recent salary slips; self-employed: latest Income Tax Return (ITR).

BUSINESS LOAN
- What it is: a collateral-free loan for business needs, repaid in fixed monthly EMIs.
- Common uses: working capital and cash-flow management, buying inventory or raw material, purchasing or upgrading equipment, opening a new outlet or expanding, marketing and growth.
- Loan amount: Rs 25,000 to Rs 50 lakh
- Repayment tenure: 6 to 60 months
- Interest rate: 12% to 18% per year (reducing balance), set by the lender
- Decision time: lenders typically decide within 2 to 3 business days after complete documents. Timelines vary and are not guaranteed.
- Typical eligibility:
  * Business operating for at least 12 months
  * Proprietorships, partnerships, self-employed professionals (doctors, CAs and others), gig workers and self-help groups can apply
  * Credit score typically 650 or above; some lenders also consider new-to-credit applicants
  * Business and office must not be on the lender's negative list
  * Not eligible: charities, NGOs and trusts
- Documents the lender may ask for: KYC (Aadhaar, passport or Voter ID, any one); PAN card; bank statements for the last 12 months; business registration proof (GST, Udyam registration or Shop and Establishment certificate, any one); business address proof (GST certificate, recent utility bill, rent agreement or Udyam registration).

HandLoan.ai only facilitates personal loans and business loans with tenures of 6 months or more. We do not offer payday loans, short-term loans repayable in 90 days or less, gold loans, home loans, car loans, credit cards or investment products. PART 6. EMI
======================================================================

EMI (Equated Monthly Instalment) is the fixed amount paid every month until the loan is repaid. Each EMI covers part of the principal and the interest on the outstanding balance.
Formula: EMI = P x r x (1 + r)^n / ((1 + r)^n - 1), where P = loan amount, r = monthly interest rate (annual rate / 12 / 100), n = tenure in months.
A longer tenure means a lower EMI but more total interest. Keeping total EMIs within 40 to 50% of monthly income is a good rule of thumb.
Customers can calculate their EMI at handloan.ai/emi-calculator/

Example EMIs at 14% per year (illustration only, excluding fees):
- Rs 50,000 for 12 months: EMI about Rs 4,489; total interest about Rs 3,872
- Rs 1,00,000 for 12 months: EMI about Rs 8,979; total interest about Rs 7,745
- Rs 1,00,000 for 24 months: EMI about Rs 4,801; total interest about Rs 15,224
- Rs 1,00,000 for 36 months: EMI about Rs 3,418; total interest about Rs 23,039
- Rs 2,00,000 for 24 months: EMI about Rs 9,603; total interest about Rs 30,462
- Rs 3,00,000 for 36 months: EMI about Rs 10,253; total interest about Rs 69,118
- Rs 5,00,000 for 36 months: EMI about Rs 17,089; total interest about Rs 1,15,197
- Rs 5,00,000 for 60 months: EMI about Rs 11,634; total interest about Rs 1,98,048
- Rs 10,00,000 for 60 months: EMI about Rs 23,268; total interest about Rs 3,96,095
Always add: "This is an estimate. Your actual EMI and APR are in the lender's Key Facts Statement."

PART 7. IMPORTANT LOAN TOPICS
======================================================================

KEY FACTS STATEMENT (KFS): a standard summary that RBI-regulated lenders must give before you sign. It lists the loan amount, interest rate, APR, all fees and charges, the repayment schedule, the cooling-off period and the lender's grievance contact. Customers should read it carefully before accepting.

CREDIT CHECK / CIBIL: to assess an application, the lender or an entity authorised by it may check the customer's credit report, only with the customer's consent. The customer is told who is checking and why. A lender's enquiry may be recorded on the credit report. HandLoan.ai does not sell credit reports.

LOW CREDIT SCORE: each lender has its own criteria. Personal loan lenders usually look for 750 or above; business loan lenders typically 650 or above, and some consider new-to-credit applicants. We cannot guarantee approval. General tips to build a healthy score: pay EMIs and card bills on time, keep credit card usage low, avoid many loan applications in a short time, and check your credit report for errors.

REPAYMENT: repay the lender directly through the channels in the loan agreement, usually auto-debit (e-NACH). HandLoan.ai does not collect repayments into its own account.

MISSED EMI: a missed or late EMI may attract penal charges as stated in the KFS and can lower the credit score. If you expect difficulty, contact your lender early to discuss options. Recovery practices are governed by the lender's fair practices code and RBI rules.

PREPAYMENT / FORECLOSURE: possible as per the lender's policy; any charges are stated in the KFS.

CANCELLING AFTER DISBURSEMENT (COOLING-OFF): digital loans have a cooling-off period, stated in the KFS, during which the borrower can exit by repaying the principal and the proportionate APR-based cost without penalty. The lender may keep a reasonable one-time processing fee if disclosed upfront. Contact the lender promptly. Details: handloan.ai/refund-policy/

WITHDRAWING AN APPLICATION BEFORE DISBURSEMENT: email help@handloan.ai with the application reference, and also contact the lender if it already has the application.

WHERE THE MONEY GOES: the lender disburses directly to the customer's own bank account. Never to a third party.

COLLATERAL: personal loans and business loans facilitated by HandLoan.ai are unsecured / collateral-free.

WHO CAN APPLY: Indian residents aged 18 or above with a regular source of income. Most lenders look for ages 21 to 60. Students or homemakers without their own income are usually not eligible on their own.

AVAILABILITY: across India, subject to the lender's serviceable locations (pin codes).
